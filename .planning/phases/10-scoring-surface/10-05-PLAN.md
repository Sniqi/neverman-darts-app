<objective>
Close the SCOR-04 verification gap: 10-VERIFICATION.md found that Plan 10-04's removal of ScorePanel.svelte's landscape font-size overrides (done to make the DS's uniform 96px/44px scale apply in both orientations, per RESEARCH.md Pitfall 4) causes the active player's 96px remaining score to be visually clipped by its own card when 3 or 4 players are active in landscape at 1024x768 (`.score-panel` scrollWidth 338px > clientWidth 324px). This plan adds a targeted, DS-consistent mitigation -- a `clamp()`-based font-size reduction for the active score, scoped ONLY to a `.compact` state (playerCount >= 3) inside the existing landscape media query -- so 2-player landscape and all portrait cases keep the untouched 96px/44px scale, while 3-4 player landscape shrinks just enough to eliminate the clipping. Replaces the never-executed manual-only checkpoint with an automated Playwright regression test at the exact failing scenario.

Purpose: SCOR-04 must hold for the app's common multi-player scenario (3-4 players is typical home-darts group size); a giant unmistakable active score is worthless if part of it is invisible.
Output: `ScorePanel.svelte` compact-mode fix; `ScorePanel.test.ts` compact-class regression tests; new `e2e/score-panel-landscape.spec.ts` proving no overflow at 3 and 4 players, 1024x768 landscape; `10-VALIDATION.md` updated to reflect the automated replacement for the manual-only row.
</objective>
